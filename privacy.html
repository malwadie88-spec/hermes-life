Privacy Policy — Hermes Life

Last updated: November 2026

Hermes Life is a private personal assistant used by a single person (its owner) on the owner's own server. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google user data

  • What is accessed: events in the owner's Google Calendar (scope calendar.events).
  • How it is used: only to show the owner their own schedule, warn about conflicts, and add or remove events when the owner explicitly asks.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is stored only on the owner's private server. Calendar data is read on demand and is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
  • No invitations: the app never adds guests or sends invitations or emails to other people.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
